SCI-MAT Extended Executive Committee Applications 2026–27
Sci-Mat is one of BMU’s leading tech clubs, offering students opportunities to explore beyond the classroom through events, workshops, and initiatives. The Extended Executive Committee helps first-year students develop practical skills, discover their interests, and collaborate with like-minded people in teams such as organising, writing, designing, promoting, and capturing moments.
 
TEAMS YOU CAN JOIN
 
 
01
OPERATIONS
Learn how a team works behind the scenes to make an event successful by planning and coordinating events and managing logistics.
 
 
02
MARKETING
Promote the club’s events and workshops, work on outreach via clubs social media and use your creative side to increase clubs’s engagement.
 
 
03
CONTENT
Be the voice of the club by creating engaging captions, write-ups, scripts and all the communication material.
 
   
  
04
DESIGN
Create eye catching posters and visual content that actually represents sci-mat and makes it stand out.
 
   
  
05
COVERAGE & EDITING
If you have a passion to be behind the camera and like to capture things by documenting and showcasing videos for social media, this is the option for you.
   
  
06
WEB DEVELOPMENT
Build, maintain, and optimize the club's official website and different technical work to ensure smooth event registrations, seamless user experience, and a strong online presence.
